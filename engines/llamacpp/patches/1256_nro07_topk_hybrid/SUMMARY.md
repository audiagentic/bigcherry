# 1256_nro07_topk_hybrid

**Status:** untested
**Plan item:** NRO07

## What it does

Replaces the HIP TOP_K route with the nasone fork's hybrid kernels: `k == 1` (and, on HIP >= 7.15, small rows) use a selection kernel, rows wider than 1024 use a parallel radix select, everything else keeps bitonic argsort. `top-k.cu` is compiled as wave64 when every HIP target is gfx11/gfx120x. `BIGCHERRY_PATCH_TRACE=1` logs one activation marker per route.

## Why

Non-CUB HIP sorted the whole row even when only a few indices are needed (MoE routing, sampling).

## Upstream

Exact port of nasone commits `7f3e1e4d0b166cb681b2c01503370e610a5b423d` and `10fdba9ae2714f5b786374c43d306e460db66a1c`, generated by `bigcherry.patch.port_diff` and verified byte-exact against the fork's file. The fleet's ROCm (7.2.4, 7.14) compiles out the small-row route (HIP >= 7.15 only).

# 1257_nro08_topk_wave32

**Status:** untested
**Plan item:** NRO08

## What it does

Makes 1256's HIP TOP_K kernels wave32-native (wave32 shuffles instead of LDS/barrier reductions) and drops the wave64 compile flag. Same routes and markers as 1256, tagged `patch=1257_nro08`.

## Why

The follow-up reduces LDS/barrier traffic; it is kept as its own patch so its effect is measured against 1256, not bundled with it.

## Upstream

Exact port of nasone commit `7f1d25f7e05cb34053d317a0f72f601d846c8662` on top of 1256, generated by `bigcherry.patch.port_diff` and verified byte-exact against the fork's file.

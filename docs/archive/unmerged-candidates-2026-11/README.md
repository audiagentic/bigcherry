# Unmerged candidates, 2026-11

Kernel candidates that were built, gated and measured on gfx1100 against release 2020.11.2 and not shipped. Each patch is the candidate's diff of its engine files, unchanged, with a short comment header. They apply to `engines/radiance/components/libr3/native/` at the 2020.11.2 sources. Method for every row: full libr3 plugin built in scratch next to the shipped plugin, resource records of every kernel compared, output bytes hashed against the shipped plugin, cold replay (256 MiB flush) ABBA with 60 launches per point on one XTX.

| Patch | What | Measured | Why it was rejected |
|---|---|---|---|
| `iq8r-ragged-half-band.patch` | A prefill whose rows are not a multiple of the 128-row band ends with a 64-row-high band instead of a full one (IQ8R tiled GEMM, plain wide-group int8 row only). | Byte-identical (41 of 41 outputs); resources: 0 records changed, 1 added (240 VGPR, 4 spilled VGPRs). Replay, five shapes, 30 tail points: 4 win, 24 regress; wins about 5% on 13312x5120 (408 to 388 us at 129 rows), loses 8 to 25% on the four smaller shapes (178 to 221 us on 6144x5120). | The half band is a second launch and a second pass over the weights. On grids that do not fill the 96 compute units (N/128 of 40 to 48 blocks) the full band is latency-bound, so a shorter band saves nothing and the launch and re-read cost 40 to 50 us; it only helps when N/128 exceeds the unit count, a gain of about 1 ms in a 126 ms first token. |
| `iqr-spin-free-producer.patch` | IQR prefill for rows above 64 as a producer kernel that decodes the whole fragment plane into scratch, then the unchanged int8 prefill body as consumer; no atomics and no spin. | Byte-identical (491 of 491 outputs); resources: 88 symbols added (producer 36 to 110 VGPR, consumer 231 to 239 VGPR), 0 spills, 0 changed. Replay, four shapes, 64 points: 15 win, 20 regress, 29 neutral; 3 to 5% faster at 65 to 128 rows, 1 to 7% slower at 192 rows and above; the IQR to int8 gap at 128 rows moved from 1.36 to 1.32 times. | The wait was not the cost: decoding the weights is the floor (about 135 us on 13312x5120 even with no waiting), and a separate producer launch adds a full write and read of the plane that no longer overlaps the K loop. The fused single-band tile that decodes in registers replaced it (shipped separately). |

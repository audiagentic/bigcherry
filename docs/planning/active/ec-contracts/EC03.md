## Boundary/positive/control lane semantics (from the contract schema, EC01)
A contract names three distinct model+workload sets that campaign_planner.py must expand into separate, identity-preserving lanes:
- `positive`: models/workloads the hypothesis claims to improve (e.g. small_m, mtp_verify).
- `controls`: models/workloads that must NOT regress (e.g. normal decode/prefill).
- `boundary`: a swept dimension (e.g. physical_m: [1,2,3,4,8,16,32,64,128]) probing the trigger's actual envelope, not just the expected winning point.

## Validation

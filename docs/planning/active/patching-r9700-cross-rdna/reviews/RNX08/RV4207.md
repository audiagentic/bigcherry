# Elementwise/quantize launch volume and PLE host copies

## Notes

Source: flashnext-long-ctx-decode-3 kernel_trace census; flashnext-long-ctx-profile-1 copy stats.

## Findings

Decode census per MTP step per GPU: 1283 elementwise launches (bin_bcast add/mul, unary sigmoid/silu, scale, get_rows, cpy/concat/set_rows/fill/repeat) ~2.1-2.4 ms kernel time; 640 activation quantize_q8_1 launches ~1.4-1.6 ms; 347 copyBuffer launches. H2D: ~23 copies per token during decode and 4.7 s across a 47K prefill (PLE gathers from the CPU-resident per_layer_token_embd IQ4_NL table, 160 x 320M); check whether the same rows are copied to all three tensor-split ranks.

## Conclusion

Microfusion value is dominated by launch count; prioritize fusions that remove whole elementwise/quantize launches per layer (e.g. fold quantize into producers, scale+add chains), and the PLE per-rank duplication check, before kernel-speed tuning of individual small ops.

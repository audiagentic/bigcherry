# GPT diagnosis: 1332 text divergence, strided FA mask, decode cost

## Notes



## Findings

1. Compact causal filter is algebraically equivalent to ADD(sel, kq_mask) for kq_mask in {0,-inf}: sentinel clamp cannot resurrect a dead slot, duplicate (q,k) slots write identical zeros, exp/step of -inf is exact. Not equivalent for finite non-zero mask biases (verify once by dumping unique kq_mask values).
2. Strided FA mask (row length GGML_PAD(n_kv+n_sel,256), relaxed assert mask->nb[0]==type size) is NOT generically valid: HIP tile and MMA f16 use nb[1]-derived stride (correct), vec with Q==1 is correct, but vec with Q>1 reads maskh[j*ne11+i] assuming nb31 == ne11*sizeof(half) (fattn-vec.cuh ~260-283) - the first R-n_kv keys of query j>=1 read the previous query's dump/padding region. Removing the materialising ADD also exposes the strided view to the meta backend directly. Highest-ranked root cause for divergence/instability.
3. Equivalent masks do not imply bit-identical text: chunking changes Q->ne[1] and so FA kernel/config selection and reduction structure; a deterministic divergence can be a near-tie. Run-to-run instability cannot be explained that way and points at the strided mask / meta transfer / FA nondeterminism.
4. Decode cost hypothesis confirmed: K=2 chunked every 2-8 token verify batch into two FA calls plus mask build. v7 pattern is coherent with 1295 returning before the chunk path above 32768 cells.
5. Predicate n_tokens >= max(K,9) gives exactly K non-empty chunks, but the scheduler still sees two graph classes (dense-small, fixed-K); both must fit the reservation with no realloc after execution starts - otherwise pre-reserve/warm both classes. Regression sequence after reserve: 1,2,8,9,K-1,K,K+1,n_ubatch with no scheduler reallocation.
6. Differential test: CPU oracle of per-query visible cell sets (dense vs compact) plus emulation of the strided read (raw[q*R+k] vs vec2 raw[q0*R+j*n_kv+k]).

## Conclusion

Next experiment: restore a per-chunk materialised contiguous mask (keep compact filter and fixed-K topology) and re-check identity/stability; if it returns, the fault is the strided mask path, and the ggml.c assert relaxation is removed from 1332. Finding 5 matches the observed no-MTP crash on b-chunk7 (dense 4-token tail after chunked prefill segfaults in ggml_backend_meta_graph_compute): 1332 must make both graph classes fit the reservation. No promotion until the no-MTP sequence passes and logits are compared against the dense path.

# Two RX 7900 XTX in tensor parallel under libr3, then the drafter on the R9700

## Description

One XTX holds the MXFP4 27B with 6.4 GiB to spare; the production goal is two XTXs (48 GiB) for larger models and for prefill beyond one R9700, with the drafter on a third card (RR03). libr3 builds radiance's all-reduce units (r4d_ar_*), but none has run on gfx1100: they use peer stores, the s_wait_storecnt rewrite and, for the lossy wire, the fused quantisers. The owner's earlier gfx1100 experiment (github.com/mkadrlik/vllm-radiance-p2p, copyable per the owner, 2026-10-09) has all-reduce extensions (radiance_ar_ext.hip, radiance_ar_quant_ext.hip), a router GEMM and a note on an MTP deadlock under graph capture; it needed IOMMU off or an ACS override for peer access.

## Steps

1. Check peer access between the two XTXs on Brutus with radiance's own probe (--p2p on must start or say why); record IOMMU/ACS state. 2. Serve the MXFP4 27B with --tp 2 --p2p auto on HIP devices 0,1 through libr3: load, health, the two smoke prompts, text against the one-card run. 3. If peer access is refused, run --p2p off (pinned host exchange) and record the cost. 4. Per-op profile at TP2: share of the all-reduce ops against GEMMs; compare --tp-wire exact against wht6. 5. rad-kbench the r4d_ar_* rows on two cards if the checker supports a two-device case; otherwise text identity and KLD against the one-card run. 6. Prefill and decode medians at TP2 against one XTX and against the R9700. 7. Read docs/fast-reduce-mtp-deadlock.md from the owner's fork before turning the drafter on at TP2; then --num-speculative-tokens auto. 8. RR03: the drafter on the R9700 (libr4d kernels there, libr3 on the XTXs), which needs two kernel libraries resolved per device.

## Effort & Risk

Large. Risks: peer access on consumer cards (IOMMU/ACS), the all-reduce units' wait-counter rewrite being wrong on gfx11 in a way a single card never exercises, and the known deadlock under graph capture with the drafter.

# Attribute B1 measurement-context sensitivity: cache residency vs eviction preconditioning

## Description

HI34 B1 (2026-08-17, closed per GPT verdict) falsified measurement-context invariance: four replicated material median-crossover winner reversals (all blas<->mmvf), plus 63 cold-arm fail-closed native-instability rejections. But the absolute shifts were not uniformly in the eviction direction (some candidates faster under flush), so B1 established MEASUREMENT-CONTEXT SENSITIVITY without attributing causation to L2/Infinity-Cache residency. This item produces that attribution — or explicitly fails to — via a bounded diagnostic matrix, so that B2 (HI24 step 7) calibrates against a settled measurement context and the HI35 re-baseline uses settled semantics.

Purpose is NOT 'prove cache causation'. Purpose: reach a defensible MEASUREMENT-POLICY DECISION with orthogonal controls. The item must not pass because one explanation looks plausible; its closure condition is a supported policy decision, one of exactly three terminal states.

## Steps

- Bounded diagnostic matrix on Brutus (same GPU/build as B1, lps=1, unblocked, hot brackets around the experiment): the 4 B1 material crossover signatures (2bd325e861971d70687bec3ba6667f48, 38ee4d7037e60cdb0405ec302b191ed2, ee8a2c18d47bd4b7a9244a1dbe7454dd, f33c999707eec598f5fd24618ca00a29) PLUS at least 4 matched negative controls that did not move in B1, covering similar blas/mmvf families and latency/working-set ranges. Positive-only sampling would make almost any intervention look explanatory.
- Intervention arms: size sweep 128 / 256 / 512 MB (locked in RV47) plus a small-size control (NOT treated as the sole DVFS control - a smaller buffer simultaneously changes cache coverage and the amount/duration of GPU work, so it does not orthogonalize those effects), plus an evict-then-rewarm arm: 256 MB eviction -> synchronize -> UNTIMED candidate/operand rewarm -> synchronize -> timed candidate. The rewarm preserves much of the preceding eviction workload while deliberately restoring hot residency; it is not mathematically perfect (the rewarm itself affects clocks) but combined with the size response and device telemetry is far more diagnostic than a tiny-buffer arm alone.
- Implement the pre-sample intervention in hip-autotune-tuner.cu as a SINGLE internal enum NONE / EVICT / EVICT_REWARM (per GPT sign-off condition): env vars map into that one mode, invalid combinations are unrepresentable rather than runtime-rejected, and the resolved mode is recorded in the artifact header. Threaded through time_candidate like the existing flush seam, with contract tests pinning: kernel ordering eviction -> sync -> untimed rewarm -> sync -> timed window; forced lps=1 for EVICT and EVICT_REWARM; default NONE.
- Collect HI60-style SMI clock/thermal/power telemetry around every arm and apply HI60's stable/corrected/unresolved treatment - reuse that vocabulary and tooling, do not invent a new telemetry system.
- Evaluate with tools/residency_gates.py (or an extension of it): crossover penalties and absolute candidate shifts per signature per arm; native-unstable rejection counts per arm.
- Record the terminal decision in HI34 notes + COVERAGE_AUDIT.md, whichever of the three states results, and set the follow-on policy for B2/HI35 accordingly.

## Detailed Solution & Technical Design

TERMINAL DECISIONS (exactly one, mandatory):

1. CACHE-SUPPORTED: intervention evidence identifies residency as material enough that measurement-context design must be reopened before B2 (e.g. crossover tracks eviction size with onset/saturation, collapses under evict-then-rewarm, and telemetry is clean).
2. PRECONDITIONING-SUPPORTED: B1's 256 MB mechanism perturbed selection principally through non-cache state (crossover independent of rewarm, tracks clock/power/thermal shifts, or size response flat while telemetry moves); hot/default context remains the policy baseline; B2 may proceed.
3. UNRESOLVED-MATERIAL: material inversions persist but cause cannot be separated; do NOT silently choose flush=0 or flush=1 as 'truth'; measurement-context design stays blocked and a narrower follow-up is required.

ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA (locked before running):
1. Reproducibility prerequisite: hot bracket runs show no material crossover on the diagnostic signatures. If a B1 positive cannot reproduce in the same basic hot context, causal attribution is INCONCLUSIVE.
2. B1 reproduction: at 256 MB the four B1 inversions must reproduce IN DIRECTION against both hot brackets. Failure to reproduce means B1 remains historically valid evidence of context sensitivity but those four cannot be used as stable causal probes.
3. Size-response evidence: record crossover penalties and absolute candidate shifts at 128/256/512 MB. Do NOT require naive monotonicity (cache replacement and DVFS are nonlinear); what matters is whether an onset/saturation pattern exists and whether 256/512 give materially consistent ranking behavior.
4. Rewarm discriminator: if eviction causes the inversion but eviction->rewarm collapses it back to the hot ordering, strong support for residency/cache-state causation; if the inversion survives rewarm, cache residency alone is insufficient and preconditioning/fabric/DVFS remains implicated.
5. Telemetry discriminator: no causal 'cache' conclusion is permitted when the relevant runs have unresolved clock-drift evidence or when the crossover tracks a systematic clock/power/thermal state change (HI60 fail-closed vocabulary).
6. Negative controls: the matched non-B1 signatures must be reported under EVERY intervention; if many controls flip too, that points to a global preconditioning artifact rather than shape-specific cache residency.
7. Cold-instability accounting: record native-unstable rejection counts by 128/256/512 and rewarm control. B1's count of 63 is a dependent variable: does instability scale with intervention strength, survive rewarm, or disappear?
8. Terminal decision recorded per the three states above; closure requires the supported policy decision, not plausibility.

SEQUENCE CONTEXT (locked by GPT B1 close-out): B1 close -> THIS ITEM -> HI35 re-baseline under settled semantics -> B2 -> Slice C. B2 non-semantic scaffolding may proceed in parallel, but no B2 acceptance experiment or retention-default freeze before this item closes.

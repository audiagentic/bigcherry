# Tuner measured inside HIP graph capture, poisoning the capture

## Notes

Follow-up worth considering under HI14 (graph capture verification): tuning is currently *skipped* under capture rather than deferred. A signature only ever seen inside a captured graph will never be tuned at all. Options are to tune it on a side stream outside the capture, or to have the tuning run disable graphs wholesale. The warning makes the gap visible but does not close it.

## Findings

Found on gfx1100 2026-08-05, the last defect blocking a complete sweep, and the only one of the five that is not an eligibility bug.

The tuner times candidates with `hipEventRecord`, synchronisation and `hipEventElapsedTime`. Under an active HIP graph capture those calls are not *executed*, they are *recorded* into the capture, and the first one invalidates it. The failure then surfaces somewhere else entirely, as `ROCm error: operation failed due to a previous error during capture` at `ggml-cuda.cu:114` — pointing at whoever ends the capture rather than at the tuner that broke it. 810 signatures tuned cleanly before it hit.

Neither the dispatch layer nor the tuner had any notion of capture state. The candidate descriptor carries a `graph_safe` flag, but nothing consulted capture status, and `graph_safe` is a different question anyway: it asks whether a candidate may be *launched* under capture, not whether the tuner may *measure* under capture. The answer to the second is always no, for every candidate.

This is a close relative of the unbounded-recursion bug already recorded in HANDOFF: both are cases where the tuner runs in a context that cannot support it, and both present as a fault with no bigcherry frame in view.

## Conclusion

**Fixed and verified.** `ggml_hip_stream_is_capturing(lc.stream)` is checked in the TUNE branch of `ggml_hip_dispatch_resolve`; when a capture is active the native binding is kept and no measurement is attempted. A query failure fails closed (treated as capturing) — not tuning costs one signature's measurement, tuning inside a capture costs the graph.

A `std::call_once` warning states that some launches went unmeasured and points at `GGML_CUDA_DISABLE_GRAPHS=1` for a complete run. Warned once rather than per launch, since the fact that matters — "some signatures were not measured" — needs saying exactly once, and coverage already reports them as undispatched.

Note `cudaStreamIsCapturing` is not aliased in `vendors/hip.h`, so `hipStreamIsCapturing` is called directly, consistent with the tuner's existing use of raw `hip*` event calls.

With this fixed the **full sweep completes: 1151 signatures tuned, 0 failures, exit 0, 2/2 backends passed.**

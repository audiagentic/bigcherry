# HI17 native-versus-forced same-input parity evidence review

## Notes

The latest external review correctly rejected the earlier evidence as insufficient because it lacked same-input parity and a repeatability control. A rebuilt production tune target was exercised on Brutus with the exact four BLAS representatives (SGEMM, GemmEx, strided-batched, pointer-batched) on gfx1030, gfx1100, and gfx1201, with graphs disabled, HIP_LAUNCH_BLOCKING=1, 100 final interleaved samples, and all experiment/compute overrides unset. Three independent 12-cell runs were collected.

## Findings

All 36 cell-runs completed with measured=2, measurement_failure=false, native and forced candidates status=ok, samples=100, samples_us length=100, nmse=0, max_abs=0. Native and forced telemetry matched on effective API, call family, BLAS metadata, workspace, and signature. Paired timing differences stayed small in the prescribed representatives per run; a few gfx1100 cells showed run-to-run frequency/noise variation and were retained as native rather than promoted. The tuner field is_native_twin remains intentionally unpopulated; the three independent runs are empirical repeatability evidence only and do not claim HI24 double-native implementation.

## Conclusion

The review's same-input correctness and interleaved timing evidence gate is now materially addressed for the exact HI17 representative matrix. Do not implement HI24's native twin in this HI17 slice. Keep HI17 open until the branch's authoritative timing-parity acceptance is reconciled with the earlier large-K non-parity evidence and the external reviewer confirms that the three-run native repeatability control is sufficient. Keep the separate HI24 double-native hook open.

# Serialize reciprocal journal admission within one lease

## Notes



## Findings

GPU lease proves ancestor ownership, not exclusivity between sibling processes within that lease. Both owners could scan safe journals before either publishes an active record. Recovery can race with admission or another recovery.

## Conclusion

Use one host-local nonblocking flock at the configured shared power root; serialize prepare/apply/restore/recover and native owner lifetime. Fail closed on unavailable or busy lock; do not replace lease or durable journals. Add card-free contention tests.

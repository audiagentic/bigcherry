## Interpretation and limits

- **Correctness for this scenario:** 30 verified generations, no mismatched FP16 words, each sampled GPU-ready generation matches the CPU-published generation, exit 0 and completion 30/30. Confirms the later positive R9700 DMA-BUF evidence remains reproducible on the current host.
- **Not full qualification:** 3.93 MiB is below the ~10 MiB TP3 target; this is one R9700 destination, not three concurrent CPU pushes while all GPUs publish partials. It does not validate buffer close/restart, crash handling, ring wrap, exact TP3 sum, all-rank drain or production inference.
- **Timing is diagnostic only:** 840.96 µs p50 includes CPU FP16 combine, GPU arm, full-word verifier and ACK. Thirty samples do not justify a production p99 claim or an end-to-end speedup. Existing 2026-11-10 historical R9700 direct combine A/B remains a separate serial comparison.

1. SummaryVTP36 should prioritize the 2,048-token prefill critical path, not a general rewrite of tensor parallelism. Native Radiance TP3 already outperforms dual-XTX TP2 at 4, 6 and 8 concurrent streams. Its unresolved deficit is specifically MXFP4 single-prompt prefill at 2,048 tokens.The strongest source-supported explanation is that the two layouts cross the prefill-pipelining threshold at different points:TP2: Uses the plugin's default 1,024-row threshold. A 512-token prompt runs serially; a 2,048-token prompt can use the two-piece pipeline.TP3: Explicitly sets the threshold to 256 rows. Both 512-token and 2,048-token prompts can use the pipeline.At 2,048 tokens: Each pipeline operates on two 1,024-row compute pieces, but TP3 still performs exact three-rank collectives through pinned host memory, with the R9700 limited by PCIe Gen4 x4.This explains why TP2 has substantially more opportunity to improve between 512 and 2,048 tokens, while TP3 has already captured the pipeline benefit at the shorter length.
